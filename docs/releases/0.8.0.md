# Anomalyzer 0.8.0

Adds the explicit `ForecastSettings.baseline="recent-level-v1"` fallback for
forecasts with no selected trend or cycle. Existing calls default to `legacy`;
standalone analyzer recipes and Guard detector settings retain their behavior.

- Compares persistence with four fixed simple-exponential-smoothing alphas on
  bounded chronological seven-bucket holdouts, with a one-step regression gate.
  Unsupported improvement, ties and short histories retain persistence.
- Keeps baseline choice separate from robust/include handling. Smoothing all
  measured observations does not label them outliers; robust screening and
  reference protections remain distinct disclosed policies.
- Freezes historical selection before its targets and recalibrates residuals
  against that baseline. Explicit eligible lag-one history now trains on 56
  points, then calibrates on 14; other explicit models retain their boundaries.
  Current and historical selections may differ and expose separate provenance.
- Adds baseline parameters, selection windows, losses and diagnostic reasons to
  forecast models and native historical settings. JSON schemas and the pinned
  UsageTap bridge example cover the opt-in `levelBaseline` field.
- Expands nested held-out evaluation to 19 anonymous fixtures / 76 policy runs.
  Stationary noise and endpoint spikes sometimes benefit; a noisy real step
  substantially worsens, one stationary seed slightly worsens, and the declared
  random walk retains persistence. No accuracy or health guarantee is implied.

Contract names remain `forecast-v1`, `historical-anomalies-v1` and the example's
`usage-forecast-v1`. Strict consumers need the updated optional schema fields and
an exact 0.8.0 engine pin before enabling the option. A dependency upgrade alone
does not activate it. Preserve original settings/results and fingerprints for
retained findings; new engine/config runs have new provenance.
