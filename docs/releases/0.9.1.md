# Anomalyzer 0.9.1

Fixes unsupported automatic exponential trend selection that could drive
historical expectations far above observed usage. A late ramp could win an
in-sample exponential fit; robust reference replacement could then repeatedly
feed that extrapolation into later references.

- Automatic exponential fits now require training-only chronological
  confirmation against automatic none/linear selection: at least two holdouts,
  5% aggregate rolling one-step MAE improvement and wins on two thirds of folds.
  Unsupported or insufficient confirmation retains the simpler trend.
- Historical selection and calibration remain frozen before evaluated targets.
  Original observations remain visible. Forward and historical models select
  independently; explicit exponential requests remain available.
- Regression coverage includes a late ramp followed by a plateau, compound
  seasonal growth, short history, bounded fitting windows and prefix invariance.
  Anonymous chronological evaluation results have been regenerated.

Request/result contracts are unchanged. Automatic numerical selections can
change in forecast and analysis APIs that share the trend fitter. Training
confirmation does not guarantee future accuracy or validate business incidents.

UsageTap must update its statistics bundle, exact engine pins, artifact hashes
and versioned caches. Its compatibility outlier-index union includes historical
reference adjustments; it must not label the entire union as forward training
exclusions or turn reference adjustments into incidents. See the
[consumer correction notes](../usagetap-forecast-migration.md#historical-exponential-reference-correction).
