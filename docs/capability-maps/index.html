Anomalyzer capability maps

What changed, compared with what should have happened, and what can we conclude?

Start with the story map to follow an investigation. Open a capability to inspect its behavior, acceptance criteria, supporting evidence, and limits.

  • Understand a change: interactive story map — follow the user journey and choose how to use analysis in your workflow.
  • Interactive dependency map — explore dependencies and inspect acceptance coverage.
  1. Supply comparable data.
  2. Define the comparison, including expected patterns or related metrics.
  3. Find changes, including early signals and changes within unfinished days.
  4. Inspect findings and trace their supporting evidence.
  5. Assess how much the history and expected baseline support a finding.
  6. Apply your review policy to action and notification eligibility.

Use analysis through the command line, Python, or read-only agent tools. The caller owns notification delivery and operational actions.

Coverage reflects saved capability reviews. It does not measure detection accuracy on real-world data.
